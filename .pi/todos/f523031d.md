Import the scrobble history from Last.fm into the play log.

- `user.getRecentTracks` (paged, with timestamps) gives the full history with real times; `track.getInfo` `userplaycount` gives totals only.
- Songs are matched by artist + title (+ album, MBID when present), so matches will be fuzzy.
- Avoid double counting plays that were both logged locally and scrobbled: dedupe by timestamp + song.
- Open questions: a one-off import or an ongoing sync? Where to show progress and errors? How to report songs that don't match anything in the library?
