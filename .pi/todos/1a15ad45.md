Copies of the same song in the library (same recording in different folders or formats) should be flagged as duplicates.

Context:
- Play counts will recognise a song by its MusicBrainz recording ID, else artist + title + album ignoring case, else the path. This means duplicate copies share one play count, which is the intended behaviour.
- The same key could be used to find duplicates.

Open questions:
- Where does the flag show: a property/column ("Duplicate" / copy count), a filter, a badge in song details, or a list in Settings?
- What counts as a duplicate: only matching recording IDs, or also artist + title + album? Should different durations (a different edit or remaster) be excluded?
- Should one copy be preferred for playback (for example the best format)?
